## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-performance, tournament-grade esports atmosphere optimized specifically for a crisp, high-contrast light mode. Straying from the clichéd murky dark themes of traditional gaming interfaces, this aesthetic adopts an elite broadcast, tech-forward sports analytics identity: clean, tactical, intensely focused, and instantly readable under direct daylight.

The design movement combines **High-Contrast Modernism** with dynamic, kinetic **Neo-Arcade Accents**:
- Ultra-defined structural borders and pristine canvas whites replace dark visual clutter.
- Electric cyan-blue and ultraviolet pigments inject hyper-focused energy, denoting competitive status, live states, dynamic odds, and match milestones.
- Typographic tension balances dense, tabular data readouts with aggressive, modern display headlines to instill urgency, speed, and competitive authority.

## Colors

The color palette leverages a crisp optical architecture built to maintain razor-sharp legibility, vibrant focal hierarchy, and strict WCAG AAA contrast for body copy.

### Palette Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#ffffff`) & Canvas Sub-tier (`#f6f8fa`, `#edf1f5`)**: Distinct background planes that keep complex game ladders, bracket trees, and stat dashboards organized without visual fatigue.
- **Solid Inks (`#0a0d14`, `#121620`)**: Deep obsidian inks ensure headlines and primary metrics remain uncompromisingly legible against pure light backdrops.
- **Secondary Carbon (`#4b5563`)**: Used for metadata, player handles, timestamps, and secondary statistical tags.
- **Primary Electric Blue (`#0077e6`)**: Drives interaction—primary CTAs, selected bracket pathways, live broadcast toggles, and active tab indicators.
- **Secondary Ultraviolet (`#7928ca`)**: Denotes premium tiers, tournament grand finals, MVP badges, and high-tier competitive rankings.
- **Tertiary Kinetic Cyan (`#00d4b2`)**: Reserved for live events, affirmative status signals, kill feeds, and dynamic upward stat vectors.
- **Structural Stroke (`#e2e8f0`)**: Delivers crisp structural framing across all card tiers and tabular grids.

## Typography

Typography establishes an assertive broadcast pacing. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Chivo`)**: Selected for its aggressive cuts, athletic posture, and high-velocity clarity. Used in all match headings, tournament titles, and scoreboard leads. Uppercase styling is preferred for sectional headers and tournament bracket identifiers.
- **Body (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Engineered for dense data clarity. Neutral, contemporary, and geometrically balanced, it maintains zero eye fatigue during extended game recaps, roster reads, and tournament rule overviews.
- **Data & Numeric Labels (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Provides fixed-width alignment for kill-death-assist (KDA) ratios, tournament clocks, ping/latency displays, and match prize distributions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column structural grid system on desktop that shifts dynamically to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a dense 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Layout Principles
- **Density Over Open Whitespace**: As a competitive analytics and broadcast platform, utility and data density take priority over exaggerated emptiness. 
- **Modular Stat Blocks**: Bracket systems and match scoreboards rely on synchronized horizontal rows utilizing `space-sm` internal item gaps and `space-md` section segmentations.
- **Breakpoints & Adaptation**:
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: Multi-pane layout with a fixed match navigation rail, central stream/bracket viewport, and right-hand telemetry/chat sidebar.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: Sidebar collapses into a slide-over canvas; brackets unlock smooth horizontal drag panning.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single-column stack with persistent bottom-pinned match navigation and high-density tabular score strips.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy muddy shadows in favor of a crisp architectural approach built on **Crisp Contours & Tinted Low-Diffusion Elevation**:

1. **Surface Layers**:
   - **Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#f6f8fa` base view layer.
   - **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Pure `#ffffff` surface framed with a sharp `1px solid #e2e8f0` border.
   - **Level 2 (Active/Hover/Focused State)**: `#ffffff` surface with `1px solid #0077e6` and subtle blue ambient depth (`0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 119, 230, 0.08)`).
   - **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns)**: `#ffffff` surface with `1px solid #e2e8f0` and a dual-cast ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 13, 20, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 13, 20, 0.04)`.

2. **Glow & Energy Highlights**:
   Live matches, dynamic lead changes, and interactive toggles employ low-radius, high-vibrancy micro-halos (e.g., `0 0 8px rgba(0, 212, 178, 0.35)`) directly over accents rather than diffuse card drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** corner geometry (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius, `0.5rem` / `8px` for cards, and `0.75rem` / `12px` for dialogs and hero panels). 

This sharp, slightly rounded approach evokes esports hardware, monitor bezels, and tournament stage production gear. Circular forms are strictly reserved for player avatars and live broadcast notification indicators. Pill shapes are avoided for structural cards to maintain an engineered, tech-focused posture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0077e6` background, `#ffffff` bold text (`Chivo`), `4px` border radius. Hover introduces a dynamic shift to `#0066c7` with micro-glow.
- **Secondary / Tactical**: Solid `#edf1f5` background, `#0a0d14` text, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`. Hover turns background to `#e2e8f0`.
- **Special Action / Grand Final**: High-contrast gradient or solid `#7928ca` with `#ffffff` text, reserved for champion tracks, MVP voting, and tournament entry.
- **Destructive**: Crisp `#ffffff` card button with `#dc2626` text and border `1px solid #fecaca`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips (Live / In-Game)**: `#00d4b2` (12% tint background), `#007a66` label text, accompanied by an animated pulsing 6px dot.
- **Tournament Tier Chips**: Solid `#0a0d14` background with `#ffffff` micro-caps typography (`Chivo` 11px uppercase).
- **Filter Chips**: Outlined `#e2e8f0` background `#ffffff`. When selected, fill turns `#0077e6` with `#ffffff` text, completely borderless.

### Cards & Match Strips
- **Matchup Card**: Dual team module enclosed in `#ffffff` with `1px solid #e2e8f0`. Team scores rendered in `JetBrains Mono` at `18px` weight `700`.
- **Highlight Card**: Features an asymmetrical 3px primary electric blue accent bar anchored along the left border to denote priority matches.

### Inputs & Search
- **Input Fields**: Crisp `#ffffff` container, `1px solid #e2e8f0`, `0a0d14` text, placeholder `#9ca3af`. On focus: `1px solid #0077e6` paired with an electric focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 119, 230, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox (`4px` radius) with `1.5px solid #cbd5e1`. Active state: filled `#0077e6` with sharp white geometric vector checkmark.

### Esports-Specific Components
- **KDA Roster Table**: Alternating rows utilizing `#ffffff` and `#f6f8fa`. High-contrast divider lines (`#edf1f5`). Numerical readouts in `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Bracket Node**: Clean white rectangular cell with connected solid `#cbd5e1` vector lines that transition to `#0077e6` to highlight the advancing path.
